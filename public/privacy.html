Side Quest Privacy Policy

Updated October 9, 2026. This policy describes the current Side Quest app, an independent fan project maintained through the Audge93/SideQuest repository.

Local game data: your chosen player name, game/save names, scores, badges, answers and progress, selected parks, and settings are stored on your device or in your browser to save and resume games. Use a nickname; no real name is required. Accessibility preferences record your choices, not a diagnosis.

The app has no account system, advertising SDK, or app analytics SDK. It does not upload your local game data to a Side Quest server. Photo quests are instructions only: the app does not capture or upload photos or request camera, microphone, or precise location access.

Storage and deletion: local data stays until you delete it, clear browser/app storage, or remove the app. Delete an individual save from the main menu. Clear All Local Data in Settings removes the app’s saved games, player information, and preferences on this device. It cannot delete reports you separately submitted to GitHub.

Web hosting: the web version is served by Netlify. Requests for pages and app files necessarily reach the hosting provider, which may process IP addresses, browser/device information, and request logs under its own privacy policy. Local saves are not attached to these requests.

Support and external links: choosing Open Report opens GitHub with a draft containing the card ID, question, and choices, or a blank support template. Nothing is submitted until you choose to submit it there. GitHub may require an account, and submitted issues are public. Do not include private information. GitHub and other linked sites handle visits and submissions under their own policies.

Privacy questions: use the support link to contact the repository maintainer. Keep public requests free of sensitive information. This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
