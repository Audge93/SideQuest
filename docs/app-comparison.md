# Side Quest: comparison and release recommendations

Reviewed October 8, 2026. Recommendations are design judgments based on the official product pages linked below; this is not a hands-on test of competitor apps.

| Comparable app | Relevant approach | Recommendation for Side Quest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Goosechase](https://goosechase.com/how-it-works/join-experience) | Guest participation, photo/video/question missions, teams, a live activity feed and leaderboard | Keep our quick, account-free start. Add a day recap with favorite completed activities before investing in online competition. |
| [Actionbound quizzes](https://en.actionbound.com/help/article/quiz-element) | Multiple answer formats, hints and solution review | Keep answers readable until the player dismisses them. Add a way to report a questionable or outdated question. |
| [Actionbound offline preload](https://en.actionbound.com/help/article/preloading-bounds) | Downloads content before play so a hunt works without reception | Prioritize offline launch and save recovery on real phones. A loaded web page continuing to work is not the same as an offline cold launch. |
| [Let’s Roam roles](https://www.letsroam.com/help/entry/what_are_characters) | Different roles, including children, contribute to a team score | Offer family and quiet-play presets, then consider pass-the-phone turns using one device. |

## Before submitting to an app store

- Test installed iOS and Android builds: cold launch, airplane mode, background/foreground, silent mode, safe areas, saved-game recovery, and interrupted audio. Current automated coverage exercises the web build in Chrome phone viewports.
- Review larger text, screen-reader navigation, color contrast, and reduced-motion behavior on devices. Timed trivia has a 60-second alternative; Who Am I has no timer.
- Finish the rights review for backgrounds and preserved questions. Crediting the archivist and a fan-app disclaimer do not themselves establish permission. Avoid suggesting Disney endorsement in the app name, store screenshots, or description.
- Supply a real privacy policy and support contact, then check the actual data behavior against those statements and the store submission forms.
- Give the newly imported archive an editorial pass. Exact-wording deduplication does not eliminate questions asking the same fact in different words. Historical labels distinguish archive-era park facts from current operations; they do not verify every archived answer.

## Next product changes I recommend

1. Quick presets: family, quiet/line-friendly, and no-purchase activities; keep individual category controls.
2. An in-app “Report this question” action with the question ID and wording available to copy.
3. A day recap showing park visits, completed quests, minigame points, and favorite moments. Optional photo export can follow with explicit photo permissions.
4. Better repeat management and difficulty selection across normal trivia and minigames.
5. Pass-the-phone team turns before accounts or cloud multiplayer. A shared leaderboard would add moderation, privacy, and synchronization work.

Minigames are now unlimited by the user’s choice. Their points add to the active game; they do not consume hand cards, change the card streak, or earn extra 50/50 uses. Track minigame scoring separately in a future recap so families can understand where their score came from.
